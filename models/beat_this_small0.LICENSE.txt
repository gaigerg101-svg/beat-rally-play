beat_this_small0.onnx

Model: Beat This! small0 checkpoint (beat and downbeat tracker), CPJKU, Institute of Computational
Perception, JKU Linz. Code and published weights: MIT licence (text below).
Source: https://github.com/CPJKU/beat_this (package beat_this 1.1.0, checkpoint "small0" from the
official CPJKU checkpoint store, beat_this-small0.ckpt, sha256 6074be2c4d490c5f6101fcc374a1ec72ae93456e23bb6019783b849f5dc7d47b).
Converted to ONNX (opset 17, input "spect" float32 [1, T, 128], outputs "beat" and "downbeat" logits
[1, T]) with tools/reader/export_onnx.py (torch.onnx.export). ONNX sha256 08fd2652f393b04fec055ec390b0e3f97bf9ce48392d424438a42c8ebdf301dd.
The upstream README notes that some training files are copyrighted or under limited Creative Commons
licences and that users should judge whether this matters for their use. The weights themselves are MIT.
